# JWT auth

Check the token on connect, per `architecture.md` (Authentication,
Configuration) and `contract-protocol.md` (Connection, `ready`).

- Verify `token` with `jose`: HS256, `JWT_SECRET`, `exp` required.
- A missing, invalid or expired token: close with `4001`, no `ready`.
- Send `ready` only after the token is accepted.
- Do not log the token.
- `ALLOW_UNAUTHENTICATED=true` skips the check and logs a warning at startup.
- Without `JWT_SECRET` and without `ALLOW_UNAUTHENTICATED=true`, exit at startup
  with an error.
