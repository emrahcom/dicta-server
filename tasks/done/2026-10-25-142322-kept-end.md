# Kept end

Keep an `end` that arrives while a finish waits, per `architecture.md`
(Providers) and `contract-protocol.md` (Client to server).

- Server: an `end` while a finish waits is kept instead of dropped. Right after
  that finish's final, the kept `end` finishes the next utterance.
- Test the order the contract allows: audio A, `end`, audio B, `end`, then the
  final of A. B is finished right after it, without new audio.
