# Ordered finals

Ask the provider to finish at once and keep the finals in order, per
`architecture.md` (Providers) and `contract-protocol.md` (Server to client).

- Server: replace the `finishing` and `endKept` flags with an ordered queue of
  expected finals. A finish with audio since the last finish asks the provider
  at once and waits for its answer; a finish without audio is an empty final.
  Send each final when it reaches the front of the queue.
- Tests: three utterances, each with audio and `end`, all before the first
  answer, give three finals in order; an `end` without audio between them gives
  an empty final in its place.
