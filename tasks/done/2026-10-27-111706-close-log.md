# Close log

Log every connection once when it closes, per `architecture.md` (Logging).

- On close, the server logs `[<sessionId>] closed code=<code> audio=<seconds>s`
  with `console.log`: the close code, from either side, and the audio bytes the
  real-time limit counts, divided by 32000 and rounded.
- Tests: a connection that sent 2.6 seconds of audio and closed with `1000` logs
  `closed code=1000 audio=3s`; a rejected token logs `closed code=4001 audio=0s`
  after `token rejected`.
