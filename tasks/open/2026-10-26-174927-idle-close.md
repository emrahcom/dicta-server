# Idle close

Close idle connections, per `contract-protocol.md` (Closing) and
`architecture.md` (Connections).

- A connection is closed with code `1000` when the client has sent no message,
  binary or text, for 10 minutes. Pongs do not count. Every message from the
  client restarts the timer, also before `ready`.
- The close goes the same way as any other close, so the provider stream is
  closed too. The timer is cleared when the connection closes.
- The idle time is a constant, like the ping interval, and not read from the
  environment.
- Tests with mock timers, as for the finish timer: a connection without messages
  is closed with `1000` after 10 minutes, and its provider stream is closed; a
  client that keeps sending stays open past it.
