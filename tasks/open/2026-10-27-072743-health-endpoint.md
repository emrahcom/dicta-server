# Health endpoint

Answer health checks, per `architecture.md` (Health).

- In the HTTP handler, `GET` and `HEAD` on `/dicta/health` answer `200` with the
  body `ok` (no body for `HEAD`) and `content-type: text/plain`. A query string
  is allowed. Everything else still gets `404`, including other methods on that
  path.
- No token, no log line.
- README: the endpoint, in the Deploy section.
- Tests: `GET` answers `200` with `ok`, `HEAD` answers `200` without a body,
  `POST` and other paths answer `404`, and nothing is logged.
